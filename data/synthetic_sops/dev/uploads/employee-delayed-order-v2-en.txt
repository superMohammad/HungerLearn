Delayed order support triage
Synthetic training data
SidrDesk Operations | Sidr Square branch | Version 2
To: Company employee

Support desk memo. Apply the relevant procedure to late deliveries in the Sidr Square queue, such as SD-286.

1. Open the delay case
When the displayed delivery estimate is at least 12 minutes past, open a Delay case and record the order ID and last status timestamp.

2. Escalate a stalled status
If tracking has not refreshed for 8 minutes after the case opens, the support agent must route the case to the duty lead. The duty lead pages the dispatch desk.

3. Message the customer
Send the customer a status update from the case. Do not promise a new delivery time until Dispatch confirms it.

4. Reuse an open case
If the same order already has an open Delay case, add the customer's latest message there and keep its original opening time.

5. Handle a missing estimate
If the order timeline shows no delivery estimate, request the last confirmed status from Dispatch and record that the estimate is unavailable.

6. Transfer a misrouted case
If the order belongs to another branch queue, transfer the existing case to that queue with the status history attached.

7. Route a cancellation request
If the customer asks to cancel a late order, link a Cancellation Request and route it to the cancellations desk.

8. Track related delays
If several late orders share one dispatch incident, keep a case for each order and add the shared incident ID to each case.

9. Handle restored tracking
If tracking resumes during an open Delay case, record the fresh status and notify the case owner that the stalled-tracking alert can be cleared.

10. Close a recovered delivery
When the timeline confirms delivery, send the customer a closure message and record the confirmation time before closing the case.
