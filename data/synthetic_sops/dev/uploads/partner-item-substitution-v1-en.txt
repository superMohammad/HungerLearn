Item substitutions
Synthetic training data
Copper Olive Kitchen | Olive Lane branch | Version 1
To: Partner restaurant staff

Follow this checklist when a paid order such as ZN-624 contains an unavailable item.

[ ] 1. Check the preference
If the customer selected No substitutions, mark the item unavailable and notify Partner Support. Do not suggest a replacement.

[ ] 2. Request approval
When substitutions are allowed, send one replacement of equal or lower price through the order screen. Pack it only after the customer approves it there.

[ ] 3. Close an unanswered request
If the customer has not answered 7 minutes after you send the request, mark the item unavailable and notify Partner Support. Keep any remaining items in preparation.

[ ] 4. Confirm shelf availability
Before offering a replacement, check available stock and tell the prep station to hold the quantity you will offer until the request is resolved.

[ ] 5. Match the requested quantity
If a customer ordered several units, offer a replacement only when you can supply the full requested quantity at the allowed price.

[ ] 6. Record a rejected offer
If the customer rejects the offer, mark that item unavailable and send Partner Support the rejection recorded in the order screen.

[ ] 7. Handle stock loss after approval
If an approved replacement sells out before packing, report it to Partner Support and leave the item unpacked pending instructions.

[ ] 8. Handle a price difference
If the approved replacement costs less, record the lower price in the order screen and leave any payment adjustment to Partner Support.

[ ] 9. Label the approved item
After packing an approved replacement, mark the preparation slip with the actual item and retain the approval in the order record.

[ ] 10. Stop a cancelled order
If the entire order is cancelled while an offer is pending, release the held stock and stop preparing that order.
