# Clip requests for the HungerLearn demo

Generate these 12 clips with any video model you like, save each one **in this folder** (`media/clips/`) under the exact file name given, and the app will pick them up from `library.json`. Six procedures are covered: 3 for couriers, 3 for partners. Every procedure has one **don't** clip (the wrong way) and one **do** clip (the right way), as the training generator prefers a do-vs-don't video.

## Rules for every clip

| Item | Courier clips (`CL-C…`) | Partner clips (`CL-P…`) |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | **Vertical 9:16** (phone), at least 720×1280 | **Horizontal 16:9** (tablet), at least 1280×720 |
| Length | 5–7 seconds | 5–7 seconds |
| Format | MP4 (H.264), 24–30 fps | MP4 (H.264), 24–30 fps |

- **No readable text anywhere**: no subtitles, no words on screens, signs, bags, uniforms or receipts, no logos or watermarks. The app adds the Arabic on-screen text, the HungerLearn logo and the Najdi narration on top. Clip audio is discarded.
- **Realistic**, documentary style, natural light, Saudi setting (Riyadh restaurant or cafe).
- **Same people across clips** where possible: one courier (young Saudi man, plain yellow-and-brown delivery jacket with no print, insulated delivery bag) and one packing staff member (restaurant employee in a plain dark apron, hair net, gloves).
- One clear action per clip, filmed so a viewer understands it with the sound off.

## Courier: انتظار استلام الطلب من الفرع (`courier-pickup-wait`)

### 1. Late preparation: تأخر التجهيز (clause CW-02)
> «إذا بقي الطلب قيد التجهيز بعد … دقيقة من تسجيل الوصول، أرسل بلاغ «تأخر الاستلام» إلى مسؤول التوزيع.»

**`CL-C01-dont.mp4`** — don't: wait silently
> Vertical shot. A young Saudi delivery courier in a plain yellow-and-brown jacket stands at a restaurant pickup counter in Riyadh, checks his wristwatch, sighs and keeps waiting idly while kitchen staff behind him are still busy cooking. He does nothing with his phone. Natural indoor light, handheld medium shot, realistic.

**`CL-C01-do.mp4`** — do: report the delay from the app
> Vertical shot. The same courier at the restaurant pickup counter looks at his wristwatch, then calmly takes out his phone and taps on the screen to send a report, the screen turned away from the camera so nothing is readable. Kitchen staff are busy in the background. Natural indoor light, medium close-up, realistic.

### 2. Before leaving: قبل المغادرة (clause CW-04)
> «لا تغادر الفرع حتى يوجّهك مسؤول التوزيع بالمغادرة أو يسلّمك موظف الفرع الطلب.»

**`CL-C02-dont.mp4`** — don't: leave empty-handed
> Vertical shot. The courier, impatient, turns away from the restaurant pickup counter and walks out through the glass door to the street with his empty delivery bag, while a staff member behind the counter is still preparing an order. Natural daylight, handheld tracking shot, realistic.

**`CL-C02-do.mp4`** — do: wait until handed the order
> Vertical shot. The courier waits calmly at the pickup counter; a restaurant staff member hands him a closed paper bag across the counter, he nods, places it into his insulated delivery bag and only then turns to leave. Natural indoor light, medium shot, realistic.

### 3. Wrong bag: اختلاف ملصق الاستلام (clause CW-09)
> «إذا عرض عليك الموظف كيساً يحمل رقم طلب مختلفاً، اتركه عند نقطة الاستلام واطلب الكيس المطابق.»

**`CL-C03-dont.mp4`** — don't: take a bag without checking
> Vertical shot. A staff member slides a closed paper bag across the pickup counter; the courier grabs it immediately without looking at the label and walks away. The label is a small blank white sticker, nothing readable. Natural indoor light, medium shot, realistic.

**`CL-C03-do.mp4`** — do: compare, leave it, ask for the right bag
> Vertical shot. The courier holds his phone next to the small blank white label on a paper bag, compares them, shakes his head, puts the bag back on the counter and politely gestures to the staff member to bring another bag. Natural indoor light, close-up on hands then medium shot, realistic.

## Partner: تعبئة الطلبات وإغلاق الأكياس (`partner-packing-sealing`)

### 4. Sealing bags: إغلاق الأكياس (clause PP-02)
> «أغلق فتحات الأكياس بختم ظاهر. إذا كان الكيس ورقياً ولا يقبل الختم، استخدم شريط الإغلاق عند طاولة التعبئة.»

**`CL-P01-dont.mp4`** — don't: open bag goes out
> Horizontal shot. At a restaurant packing table, a staff member in a dark apron and gloves places food boxes into a paper delivery bag and pushes it toward the pickup shelf with the top still open and unsealed. Natural kitchen light, medium shot, realistic.

**`CL-P01-do.mp4`** — do: fold, then seal with tape
> Horizontal shot. The same staff member folds the top of the paper delivery bag twice, then runs a strip of plain sealing tape across the fold, pressing it firmly so the seal is clearly visible. Close-up on hands, natural kitchen light, realistic.

### 5. Hot and cold apart: فصل البارد عن الساخن (clause PP-11)
> «إذا جمع الطلب أصنافاً ساخنة وباردة، ضع كل مجموعة في كيس مستقل وألصق رقم الطلب على الكيسين.»

**`CL-P02-dont.mp4`** — don't: hot and cold in one bag
> Horizontal shot. At the packing table, a staff member puts a steaming hot meal box and a cold salad bowl with ice-cold dessert cups together into the same paper bag. Visible steam from the hot box. Natural kitchen light, medium close-up, realistic.

**`CL-P02-do.mp4`** — do: two bags, both labelled
> Horizontal shot. The staff member places the steaming hot meal box in one paper bag and the cold salad and dessert cups in a second separate bag, then sticks a small blank white label on each of the two bags. Natural kitchen light, medium shot, realistic.

### 6. Securing drinks: تثبيت المشروبات (clause PP-12)
> «إذا تضمن الطلب مشروباً، ثبّت الكوب في حامل عمودي داخل كيس منفصل وتأكد من إحكام الغطاء قبل الإغلاق.»

**`CL-P03-dont.mp4`** — don't: loose cup, loose lid
> Horizontal shot. A staff member drops a takeaway drink cup with its lid sitting loosely on top into a bag full of food boxes; the cup tilts and the lid shifts. Natural light, close-up, realistic.

**`CL-P03-do.mp4`** — do: press lid, upright holder, own bag
> Horizontal shot. The staff member presses the lid firmly onto a takeaway drink cup until it clicks, sets the cup upright in a cardboard cup holder, and places the holder inside its own separate bag. Close-up on hands, natural light, realistic.
