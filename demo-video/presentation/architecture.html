How Hunger Learn works Procedure documents → role-specific training → proven mastery → team readiness

SIMPLE
1 web app · 1 database · 1 AI SDK
COST
AI runs per document, not per learner
ACCURACY
Every item cites its exact source clause
QUALITY
Nothing is published without a human
1Understand the document
AI · AUTOMATIC · ONCE PER VERSION
  • Upload PDF, Word or a scan, in Arabic or English
  • Parse text; OCR for scanned pages
  • LLM extracts clauses per section; every quote is checked against the source
  • Clauses grouped into procedures; each stored with a hash + embedding
docproc · LangGraph · EmbeddingGemma · pgvector
2Generate training
AI · ONCE PER PROCEDURE · PER ROLE
  • Lesson, practice and exam for courier, employee or partner
  • Formats: MCQ, timed decision, spot the mistake, order steps, checklist
  • Verify: exact-quote check · LLM judge · format rules → ⚠ flags
  • Short lesson video: real clips + on-screen text
Vercel AI SDK · structured output · ffmpeg
3Human approval
ADMIN / TRAINER · PER ITEM
  • Each item shown next to its source clause; ⚠ flagged first
  • Approve · edit · reject (reject regenerates with the reason)
  • draft → verified → approved → published → outdated
  • Audit log: who, what, when, which clause version
  • Published items go to learners (4)
Next.js admin · sessions · audit trail
4Learn & prove mastery
LEARNER · PUBLISHED CONTENT ONLY
  • Lesson + video → practice → exam, on the phone
  • Confidence tap on every answer
  • Graded against the approved answer key
  • Mastered → badge and level · not yet → retry
Next.js · Arabic-first RTL · English toggle
5Monitor readiness
SUPERVISOR · ONGOING
  • % passed per procedure, branch and learner
  • Who needs retraining; who was confident and wrong
  • Ask in plain Arabic → LLM writes SQL
  • Runs on one read-only view: the AI cannot change data
Postgres view · read-only role · text-to-SQL
6New version (v2)
AI + HUMAN · ONLY WHAT CHANGED
  • Identical hash → unchanged, kept at zero AI cost
  • Embeddings find candidates; lexical check confirms the match
  • Moved clauses keep their training
  • Changed procedures go back to steps 2–3; old items retire
matching rules · per-procedure carry-over
